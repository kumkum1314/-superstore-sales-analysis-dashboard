# Superstore Sales & Business Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

Developed an end-to-end business intelligence dashboard using SQL, Python, and Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability trends, customer behavior, and regional business insights using the Superstore dataset.

The project focuses on identifying:

* High-performing product categories
* Loss-making products and sub-categories
* Customer purchasing behavior
* Regional sales contribution
* Discount impact on profitability
* Executive-level business recommendations

---

## Tools & Technologies Used

* SQL (MySQL)
* Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn)
* Power BI
* DAX
* Excel / CSV Data Processing

---

## Key Features

* Executive overview dashboard
* Profitability and risk analysis
* Customer intelligence dashboard
* Q3 sales decline investigation
* Interactive KPI cards and slicers
* Dynamic DAX measures
* Business recommendations and strategic insights

---

## Business Insights

* Technology category generated the highest profit contribution.
* Furniture category showed weak profitability due to high discount dependency.
* Consumer segment contributed the highest sales share.
* West region emerged as the strongest revenue-generating region.
* High discount levels negatively impacted overall profitability.
